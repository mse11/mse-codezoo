# CodeZoo pilot: information for parents

*(Before sending: replace everything in [square brackets].)*

Dear parent,

your child is invited to try **CodeZoo**, a free and open-source game where children learn real Python by helping animals: they put blocks together (or type code), and an animal on the screen follows the program. We are running a small pilot to find out whether children enjoy it and learn from it, before we make more of it.

## What happens

- Your child plays for about 30 minutes, at [place] on [date], or at home.
- Before and after a group of levels, the game asks 3 short questions (for example "where does the monkey stop?"). It is not a test: we only compare how many answers were right before and after, to see whether the game teaches.
- Once a day, the game asks "Would you like to play again?" with five smileys.
- A grown-up may sit nearby and take notes on what is easy or hard. They don't film or photograph anyone.

## What is recorded, and what is not

The game records on the computer or tablet only: tries per level, stars, hints used, the answers to the short questions (right or not), minutes to the first star, errors the game had, and the smileys.

**Not recorded:** your child's name, photos, voice, the programs they write, anything they type, or where they are.

After the pilot, you (or the teacher) can save this data to a file with **Export pilot data**. The screen shows what the file holds before it is saved. **Nothing is sent automatically.** You choose whether to send the file to us.

In the file, your child is a random code (the **pilot id**, like `P-7KX3MQ`, shown on the export screen) and an age band (for example 8–9). We use the files only to work out the pilot results (for example "7 of 10 children solved the first level within 5 minutes"). We publish only totals, never anything about one child.

## How long we keep it, and your rights

We keep the files until the pilot report is made, and delete them within [30 days] after that; only the totals stay. You can ask us to delete your child's data at any time, also after sending it: tell us the pilot id. Taking part is voluntary, and your child can stop at any moment.
